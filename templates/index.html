What the encrypted service calculates

The browser derives debt-to-income, requested-amount-to-income, utilization, and a capped employment-stability gap. The service calculates 5 × debt-to-income + 4 × loan-to-income + 3 × utilization + 200 × stability gap on ciphertexts. It cannot see the input values or decrypted total.
